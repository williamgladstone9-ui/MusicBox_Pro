Install BoatBox Icons

Manual install on iPhone

1. Connect to WiFi BoatBox or MusicBox.
2. Open Safari to http://musicbox.lan/ios or http://192.168.44.1/ios.
3. Open Captain or Remote above.
4. Tap Safari Share button.
5. Tap Add to Home Screen.
6. Name it BoatBox Captain or BoatBox Remote.

Old iPhone note

The iPhone 5c on iOS 10 may not support modern service-worker offline caching. That is okay: the app shell is served by the Surface over the boat WiFi. “Offline” means no internet required, not no Surface required.
